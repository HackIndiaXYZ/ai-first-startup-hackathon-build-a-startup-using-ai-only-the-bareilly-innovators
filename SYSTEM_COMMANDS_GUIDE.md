# 🌌 TITAN / Sivi AI Voice Assistant - Comprehensive Commands Guide

This document provides a full, end-to-end list of all voice commands understood by the TITAN (Sivi) AI Voice Assistant. You can trigger these commands by naturally speaking to the assistant. The commands are deeply integrated with the PC's OS, enabling hands-free control.

The system supports a mix of English and Hinglish seamlessly.

---

## 🔌 1. System & Power Control
Control the fundamental power states of your computer.

*   **Shutdown:** `"shutdown the computer"`, `"shut down the computer"`, `"band karo computer"`, `"computer band karo"`, `"pc band karo"`, `"turn off computer"`, `"turn off the pc"`
*   **Restart:** `"restart the computer"`, `"reboot the computer"`, `"restart karo"`, `"reboot karo"`, `"computer restart karo"`
*   **Sleep / Goodbye:** `"sleep mode"`, `"put to sleep"`, `"so jao computer"`, `"sleep karo computer"`, `"computer ko sleep karo"`, `"ok bye"`, `"goodbye"`, `"see you later"`, `"alvida"`, `"phir milenge"`, `"good night sivi"`
*   **Lock Screen:** `"lock the screen"`, `"lock screen"`, `"screen lock"`, `"lock karo"`, `"lock kar do"`
*   **System Status:** `"system status"`, `"pc health"`, `"check system"`, `"system kaisa hai"`, `"what time is it"`, `"current time"`, `"what is the date"`, `"aaj ki date"`, `"time kya ho raha hai"`, `"time kya hai"`, `"battery status"`, `"check battery"`
